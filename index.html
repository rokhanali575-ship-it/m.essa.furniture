OUR HERITAGE & CRAFT

Crafting Wooden Legacy In Islamabad Since Decades

At M ESSA Furniture, we transform raw, high-grade timber into timeless architectural art for luxury residences and executive spaces across Pakistan.

Located in the heart of Islamabad on Kuri Road, M ESSA Furniture has established itself as a premier destination for bespoke, handcrafted solid wood furniture. Our commitment lies in uncompromising wood selection—utilizing 100% seasoned Sheesham (Rosewood), Walnut, and Oak.

100% Solid Sheesham & Walnut

We never compromise with cheap particle board or MDF. Every frame is solid hardwood.

Bespoke Customization

Provide us your room dimensions or floorplan, and our master carvers will customize every inch to perfection.

Artisan Heritage

Hand-finished polishes, antique matte coats, and high-gloss PU coatings applied by veteran craftsmen.
